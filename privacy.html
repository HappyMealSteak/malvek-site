Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 24, 2026

Malvek does not collect, transmit, or sell any of your personal data. Everything you enter into the app — saved hosts, snippets, passwords, private keys — stays on your device. The app has no backend server and no analytics.

What's stored, and where

  • Host addresses, usernames, labels, and snippets are stored locally on your device in the app's own database. They are never transmitted anywhere except to the SSH/SFTP server you configure a host to connect to.
  • Passwords, private keys, and key passphrases are stored in your device's secure credential storage (the iOS Keychain or the Android Keystore), not in the app's regular database, and are never transmitted anywhere except as part of authenticating to the server you configured.
  • SSH keys generated in the app are created entirely on your device using Ed25519 cryptography. The private key never leaves the Keychain — Malvek has no server to send it to. Only the public key can be shared, via the system share sheet, and only when you explicitly choose to.
  • SSH keys you import from a file or the clipboard are read on-device and written straight to the Keychain. They are not copied anywhere else and are not transmitted.
  • Face ID / Touch ID, if you turn on the app lock, is handled entirely by iOS. Malvek receives only a yes-or-no result and never sees biometric data.
  • SSH host key fingerprints (used to detect if a server's identity changes between connections) are stored locally alongside your saved hosts. These are not secret and are not transmitted anywhere.

None of the above is sent to Malvek's developer or to any third party. There is no Malvek-operated server that the app talks to.

Network connections the app makes

  • To the servers you configure — when you connect to a host you've added, the app opens a direct SSH/SFTP connection from your device to that server. This traffic is between your device and your server only.
  • To Apple/Google for purchases — if you unlock the app via in-app purchase, the purchase itself is processed by Apple's App Store or Google Play, not by Malvek. Malvek receives a purchase confirmation from the platform to unlock features, per each platform's standard in-app purchase flow — this is not Malvek-specific data collection.

Data you delete

Deleting a saved host from the app also deletes its stored credentials. Uninstalling the app removes all locally stored data (hosts, snippets, credentials) from your device.

Children's privacy

Malvek is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children, since it does not collect personal data at all.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the "Last updated" date above will change accordingly. Material changes will be reflected in the app's store listing release notes.
