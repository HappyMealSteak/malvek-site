>_ Malvek

An alternative to Termius and Blink that you buy once

Malvek is an SSH and SFTP client for iPhone with no subscription, no account, and no cloud. Here is an honest look at when it is the right pick, and when it is not.

Pick Malvek if

You don't want another subscription

One payment lifts the host limit for good. The terminal, SFTP, saved scripts and SSH key generation are never gated, even on the free tier.

You don't want your keys in anyone's cloud

Malvek has no server and no account. Ed25519 keys are generated on the phone and stay in the iOS Keychain. Nothing syncs, because there is nothing to sync to.

You mostly need a solid terminal and file access on the go

A key bar with Ctrl, Esc, Tab and the arrows; first-connect host key checks like the ssh command; an SFTP browser with Unix permissions; scrollback search; and fast detection when your network drops mid-session.

Pick something else if

You need port forwarding or Mosh

Malvek doesn't do either yet. Termius includes port forwarding on its free tier, and Blink supports Mosh.

You want your hosts synced across devices

Malvek is iPhone-only and keeps everything on the one device by design. If you lose the phone, you lose its keys, the same as a hardware token. Termius Pro and Prompt sync between devices.
